The North Carolina Radio Resource Society is a proposed voluntary, unincorporated information and assistance resource for amateur radio clubs and operators across the state.

A shared resource,
with a clear purpose.

We aim to make club information easier to find, connect learners with willing volunteer Elmers, and help clubs share educational opportunities and useful experience.

The society does not govern clubs, make binding decisions for them, coordinate radio frequencies, issue licenses or direct emergency operations. Each club keeps its own leadership, policies and independence. A directory listing does not enroll a club.

Participation is voluntary. Mentor contact details should be shared only with permission. The working name and founding statement remain proposals for review by the organizing volunteers.
